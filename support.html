Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you buy. Our 3-day return policy makes it easy.

✅
3-Day Return Policy
From the date of delivery
You can return or exchange most items within 3 days of delivery. The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with all tags attached.
  1. 1Contact us on WhatsApp (+91 94426 89858) within 3 days of receiving your order.
  2. 2Share your order details and the reason for return / exchange.
  3. 3Our team will confirm eligibility and provide a return address.
  4. 4Pack the item securely with tags intact and ship it back.
  5. 5Once received and inspected, your exchange or refund will be processed within 5 business days.
⚠️ Items NOT Eligible for Return or Exchange

The following items cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances:

  • All white coloured products — due to hygiene and soiling concerns, white garments are non-returnable.
  • Items worn, washed, or damaged after delivery.
  • Products with tags removed or packaging discarded.
  • Items returned after the 3-day window has passed.
  • Sale items marked as "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.
  • Custom or altered items.
🔄
Exchange Policy
Size or colour swap
We offer size and colour exchanges subject to availability. If the replacement item is of higher value, you'll need to pay the difference. If it's lower, we'll issue store credit.

Exchange shipping: Customer bears the return shipping cost. We cover the cost of sending the exchanged item back to you on orders above Rs.499.
💰
Refund Policy
How you get your money back
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Refunds are issued to the original payment method or as store credit, as preferred.

Note: Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item sent, manufacturing defect, etc.).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, and returns.

How do I place an order? +
Browse our shop, select your product and size, then click "Order on WhatsApp". This will open a pre-filled WhatsApp message to our team who will confirm your order, address, and payment details.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfers, and Cash on Delivery (COD) in select pincodes. Payment details are shared via WhatsApp after your order is confirmed.
How long does delivery take? +
Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours of payment confirmation. Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days depending on your location. Express delivery may be available on request.
Is there free delivery? +
Yes! Free delivery on all orders above Rs.1999. For orders below this amount, a shipping charge of Rs.99–Rs.149 may apply depending on location.
Can I return white coloured products? +
No — white coloured products are strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable. This is due to hygiene and soiling concerns. Please refer to the size guide before purchasing white garments and contact us on WhatsApp if you need help choosing the right size.
What if I receive a wrong or defective item? +
We sincerely apologise! Contact us immediately on WhatsApp with a photo of the item and your order details. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no extra cost to you, including return shipping.
Do you have a physical store? +
Yes! Visit us at Shanmuga Mens Wear, Vaniyan Kovil Street, Thirupathur, Sivagangai District, Tamil Nadu — 630211. Open Monday to Sunday, 9am to 9pm. You are welcome to browse the full collection in person.
How do I track my order? +
Once your order is dispatched, we will share the tracking number and courier details via WhatsApp. You can track your parcel directly on the courier's website.
Can I cancel my order? +
Yes, you can cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it by contacting us on WhatsApp. Once the order is dispatched, cancellation is not possible — but you can initiate a return after delivery.
